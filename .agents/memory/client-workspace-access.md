---
name: Internal client workspace
description: Access boundary and asset-handling decisions for client records and materials.
---

The client workspace is for signed-in company staff only. Clients do not have portal accounts or direct access. Management users with project-management permission can create and maintain records; employees can read them.

**Why:** the user chose an internal staff workspace instead of a client-facing portal.

**How to apply:** keep client profile and material APIs behind company-scoped staff authentication, and keep client files protected by the same company boundary. Do not add client logins or external sharing without an explicit request.
